# Examen civique trainer

A free study tool for the French **examen civique**, the civics test required for the *carte de séjour pluriannuelle* (CSP) and the *carte de résident* (CR).

**Use it online:** open the GitHub Pages link shown on the right of this page (under "About"), or download `index.html` and double-click it. It runs in your browser and needs no account or installation.

## What it does

- All the official questions from the Ministry of the Interior's published lists: 191 for the CSP and 209 for the CR, plus 36 practice scenarios ("mises en situation").
- After each answer you say how sure you were. Spaced repetition then brings questions back at the right time: tomorrow if you guessed, in a week if you were fairly sure, in three weeks or more if you knew it.
- Every correct answer has a short explanation in English and a link to the official source it was checked against.
- **Focus** on what you need: due reviews, questions you have not seen yet, shaky ones, or ones you got wrong while sure, for CR, CSP or a single theme.
- **Mock exam:** 40 questions in 45 minutes, marked at the end with pass or fail at 32, in the official mix of themes and scenarios.
- Several players on one device, and a "Test a friend" mode that does not touch your own scores.

The questions are in French, as in the real exam. The interface and explanations are in English.

**En français :** utilisez la traduction de votre navigateur (dans Chrome ou Edge : clic droit, « Traduire en français »). L'interface et les explications seront traduites ; les questions et les réponses restent dans leur version officielle en français.

## Where your scores are kept

Only on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there is no tracking. In Chrome or Edge on a computer you can also save your scores to a file you choose, which survives clearing the browser and can be moved to another computer. In other browsers, use the backup button now and then.

## Found a mistake?

Please [open an issue](../../issues) with the question and the source that shows the right answer.

## Disclaimer

I made this for my own revision. If it is useful to you, use it however you like. It is an unofficial study tool with no link to the French Ministry of the Interior or any exam centre.

There are no guarantees. The explanations were drafted with AI and checked against official sources, but mistakes are possible, and the wrong options and practice scenarios were invented for practice. Whether you pass or fail the real exam is your responsibility, so check anything that matters against the linked source.
