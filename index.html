How it works

Right and sure: marked known and brought back after three weeks, then at longer gaps. Right and fairly sure: back in a week, and it stays weekly until you answer it as sure. Right but guessed: treated as not known, back tomorrow. Wrong: repeated at the end of the session and again tomorrow. Wrong while sure is flagged so the explanation gets a careful read.

About

Made by Aneirin Wenner, built with Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant. Version .

I made this for my own revision. If it is useful to you, use it however you like. It is an unofficial study tool and has no link with the French Ministry of the Interior or any exam centre.

No guarantees: the answers and explanations were drafted with AI and checked against official sources, but mistakes are possible, and the wrong options and practice scenarios are invented for practice. Whether you pass or fail the real exam is your own responsibility, so check anything that matters against the source linked under each answer.

Knowledge questions: official lists published by the Ministère de l'Intérieur (Direction de l'intégration et de l'accès à la nationalité) on formation-civique.interieur.gouv.fr, whose content is offered under the Licence Ouverte / Etalab 2.0. Retrieved 11 November 2026.
